Signet only · nothing to sideload

Installing the APK: Android blocks apps from outside the Play Store — when you open the file, allow installs from your browser, then continue.

Using more than one: your account is the same everywhere, but your keys are not — they're generated on the device you're using and stay there, in the phone's keystore or encrypted in that browser. The server never holds anything that can spend, so a second device (or the browser, after the app) starts empty until you import your recovery phrase there.
