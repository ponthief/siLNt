Features
Reusable addresses without the privacy tax

Reuse one ordinary Bitcoin address and leak your history; generate new ones forever and drown in bookkeeping. Silent Payments end that trade-off.

🔇

Silent Payments

Publish one address. Each sender derives a different on-chain output, so nothing links your payments — yet only you can spend them.

Reusable & unlinkable
✉️

BitMail

Hand someone alice@mydomain.net instead of a 116-character string. Resolves over DNS.

DNS-backed
🤝

Collaborative PayJoin

Pay another member with a transaction you both put inputs into, breaking the "one owner per transaction" assumption.

Within your circle

Plus: coin control with dust flagging · biometric or PIN lock, and a duress PIN that wipes this device's keys · Lightning and on-chain ↔ Lightning swaps coming.

Who it's for. Thrilla is built for a known circle sharing one LNbits instance: members pay each other by PayJoin, and everyone shows the outside world a Silent Payment address that's safe to publish. Your keys are your responsibility — no one can move your bitcoin, or recover it if you lose your backup.
