Three steps
From nothing to your first Silent Payment

About five minutes, most of it waiting for a confirmation.

1

Create an account

Each device is confirmed by an email link from — check spam. The account gets you in; it is not what holds your coins, and a password alone can never reach them.

2

Make a wallet and back up the phrase

Keys are derived on your device — write the twelve words down. On the Android app you can see them again later under Settings → Security, behind your PIN or fingerprint, so a mistyped word is fixable. That only helps while you still have the phone: lose or wipe it with no written copy and the coins are gone.

3

Get free coins and send some

Take Signet coins from the faucet ↗, then pay your own Silent Payment address and watch a brand-new output appear for it. That is the whole idea, in one transaction.

What carries over, and what doesn't

What you learned

Everything. The mainnet build is the same wallet against a different chain — nothing about sending, receiving, coin control or backups changes.

Same app
🔑

Not your keys

Signet keys are Signet keys. Mainnet starts with a fresh wallet and a fresh recovery phrase — back that one up properly, because it is guarding something.

Start again
🪙

Not the coins

Signet coins cannot be moved to mainnet and are worth nothing. There is no exchange rate and nothing to lose — which is the point of practising here.

Worthless on purpose
Heads up When you do move to mainnet, send yourself a small amount first and spend it back out. A test you can afford to lose beats a first transaction you can't.
